# MARS SCROLLS-221bBAKER-Rides-Third-with-SHERLOC-WATSON
A characterization of the over 16000 images produced by the MARS PERSERVERANCE ROVER SCIENCE CAMERAS SHERLOC-WATSON , MICRO IMAGER and SUPERCAM from sol 11 to sol 1600
Mars has witnessed abundant dynamics and catastrophe over the more recent past than is currently accepted.  Air strikes located in the JEZERO study area bombarded a city that included a vast funerary complex and possibly a mausoleum.  Subsequently many multi species graves and tombs have been exposed through what appears to be a post impact surgical excavation of the sediment intentionally. This could mean one of three scenarios: first,  a secret space mission from Earth took on the vast project to expose the archaelogical study area with heavy equipment specifically designed to retrieve target samples or secondly, an unidentified extraterrestrial group with the technology required to accomplish such a task undertook the project of excavation OR a resident population currently occupies Mars and the sediment has been exposed and maintained. Either way the results demonstrate a concerted effort to reveal the sediments as aspects of our combined mutual past that we are entirely unaware of.
